I honestly think the "Programming Insights" square post was the easiest one to finish because once I had the four boxes set up, I just had to type in the text. For branding, I used a consistent color scheme of dark grey and tan, and I made sure to include my initials logo on the banner and the promo flyer. To make sure people could actually read it, I used strong contrast between the background and the text colors. I handled visual hierarchy by making the main titles really big and bold so they stand out more than the smaller details. I learned that social media graphics need to be clean and simple, or they just look cluttered and confusing. This project mostly showed me how to organize information so it's easy for someone to look at quickly.
