Reflection:
I designed Calendro to help students and professionals see exactly how much time they spend on work versus distractions, giving them a clear tool to improve their focus. My target users are anyone who feels overwhelmed by digital devices and wants to build better habits through honest time-tracking. I chose a clean layout with simple gray tones to keep the interface calm and professional, while using a bright orange color for important buttons so they are easy to find. A major challenge was making sure the dashboard didn't look too crowded while still showing the weekly and monthly progress at a glance. If I had more time, I would add colorful charts to show productivity trends and would polish the design a lot more.
