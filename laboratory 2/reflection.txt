My branding was inspired by modern minimalist designs because I wanted something clean and very simple. I chose neutral colors like dark greys and soft creams since I prefer darker tones. The hardest part was designing the logo, especially combining my initials, “D” and “T,” into a simple geometric shape without making it look messy. I also had some difficulty choosing a font that matched the blocky style of the logo, but Inter ended up working well. For now, I plan to use this branding on my GitHub profile, and later on, I want to apply it to a personal portfolio website to showcase my future coding projects.
