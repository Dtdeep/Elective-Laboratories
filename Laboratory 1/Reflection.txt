In creating my presentation, I found the design principle of Alignment most useful because it helped me organize my skills and background in a clean and professional way, avoiding a random or messy layout. The easiest part of Canva to use was its drag-and-drop feature, which made it simple to move and arrange elements like my programming icons. The hardest part was working with Canva’s limited customization and output formats, especially when aiming for more advanced or professional designs. Through this experience, I learned that visual communication is just as important as the content itself, since good design makes information easier to understand and remember. I also realized that using Proximity to group related items, such as contact details, greatly improves clarity and organization.
